Research Interest 01

Quantum Simulation & Many-Body Physics

I develop methods to engineer and prepare correlated quantum states in programmable quantum platforms, and investigate emergent many-body phenomena in quantum simulators, with a current focus more on interacting Rydberg atom arrays.
