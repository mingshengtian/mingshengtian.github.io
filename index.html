About

I am a postdoctoral scholar and Eberly Research Scholar in Atomic, Molecular, and Optical Physics at Pennsylvania State University, working with Bryce Gadway. My research focuses on quantum simulation, quantum information science, quantum metrology and entanglement, quantum optics and atomic physics, and AI for quantum science and technologies.

Research Interests

  • Quantum simulation and quantum information science
  • Quantum metrology and entanglement
  • Quantum optics and atomic physics
  • AI for quantum science and technologies
