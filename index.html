About关于我

I am a theorist working at the interface of quantum information science and quantum many-body physics. My research explores how controllable quantum systems can be used to study complex many-body phenomena, and how entanglement, a central quantum resource, can be characterized and harnessed. My work follows two directions: developing theoretical methods to understand emergent quantum correlations and entanglement in many-body systems, and proposing experimentally feasible protocols for quantum systems engineering, including Hamiltonian design, state preparation, and measurement.

你好，欢迎来到我的主页！我目前是宾夕法尼亚州立大学的博士后，研究方向是原子分子光物理（AMO）。

主要从事量子信息科学与量子多体物理交叉领域的理论研究，关注如何利用可控量子体系研究复杂的多体物理现象，以及如何表征和利用量子纠缠这一核心资源。具体包括两个方向：一是发展理论方法，研究多体系统中涌现的量子关联与纠缠；二是量子系统的设计，提出实验上可行的制备与测量方案。

Research研究方向

Quantum Simulation and New Phases of Matter量子模拟与新奇物态

System engineering and many-body physics系统设计与多体物理

Programmable quantum platforms offer an unprecedented opportunity to build complex many-body systems from the bottom up. In close collaboration with experimental groups, I design new control protocols that expand the range of Hamiltonians these platforms can realize, and use them to explore strongly correlated, topological, and nonequilibrium phases of matter.

可编程量子平台让我们可以像搭积木一样，自下而上地构建复杂的多体系统。我与实验组紧密合作，设计新的调控方案，不断拓展这些平台能够实现的哈密顿量，并借助它们探索强关联、拓扑以及非平衡的物质相。

Quantum Information量子信息

Entanglement generation, characterization, and applications纠缠的产生、刻画与应用

Entanglement is both a hallmark of complex quantum systems and a central resource for quantum technologies. I study how multipartite entanglement can be generated and controlled, develop experimentally accessible methods to characterize it, including approaches based on machine learning, and explore how it can be harnessed to enhance measurement precision.

量子纠缠既是复杂量子系统最鲜明的特征，也是量子技术的核心资源。我关注多体纠缠如何产生与调控，发展实验上切实可行的纠缠刻画方法（包括基于机器学习的方法），并探索如何利用纠缠来提升测量精度。
