About Me

I am a postdoctoral scholar in AMO physics at Pennsylvania State University, working at the interface of quantum metrology, quantum many-body physics, and quantum information science. My research explores how quantum coherence and entanglement can be created, controlled, and exploited across both continuous-variable optical systems and discrete programmable quantum platforms.

My work combines theoretical analysis, numerical simulation, and machine-learning methods for quantum science.
